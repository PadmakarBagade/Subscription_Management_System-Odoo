BRIGHTLOOP SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - VIDEO DEMO SCRIPT
Estimated Duration: ~3 to 4 Minutes

- URL: http://localhost:5173/login
- Note: The login screen features one-click demo buttons for all 3 roles.
  * Admin:    admin@brightloop.com    / Admin@Brightloop2026!
  * Staff:    staff@brightloop.com    / Staff@Brightloop2026!
  * Customer: john.doe@example.com    / Customer@Brightloop2026!

================================================================================
PART 1: INTRO & ADMIN WORKSPACE (~75 seconds)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN] Navigate to http://localhost:5173/login
[VOICEOVER]:
"Welcome to Brightloop — an enterprise-grade Subscription and Recurring Billing 
Management platform inspired by Odoo. In this walkthrough, we will demonstrate 
the core workflows across all three user roles: Administrator, Staff, and Customer."

[ON SCREEN] Click the 'Admin' one-click demo button, then click 'Sign in'.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Let's begin by logging in as the System Administrator."

[ON SCREEN] Land on Admin Dashboard (Executive Financial Workspace).
- Hover over the 4 KPI cards: Total Customers, Active Subscriptions, Total Invoiced, MRR.
- Scroll to Monthly Collections Trend area chart and Revenue by Plan chart.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Here on the Executive Dashboard, admins get real-time financial intelligence: 
recurring cash flow, active subscription counts, the 12-month rolling collections 
trend, and revenue contributions grouped by subscription plan."

[ON SCREEN] Click the collapse toggle button at the bottom of the left sidebar.
- Move cursor over collapsed icons to highlight quick tooltips.
- Click expand button to restore the sidebar.
[VOICEOVER]:
"The sidebar is fully responsive and collapsible with tooltips and persisted state, 
maximizing dashboard workspace."

[ON SCREEN] In Sidebar, click 'Customers' (Customer Directory).
- Point cursor to the top KPI cards: 'Total Receivables (Dues)', 'Accounts with Dues'.
- Click 'With Dues Only' filter tab to highlight customers with pending balances.
- Click 'View Statement' on Acme Innovations or Nexus Digital Systems.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Next, in the Customer Directory, we see full receivables visibility. Active 
accounts with pending dues appear first, complete with registered GSTINs. 
Opening 'View Statement' reveals the customer's total outstanding balance, 
their active contracts, and issued invoices."

[ON SCREEN] In Sidebar, click 'Users & Staff'.
- Click 'Create Staff Account' button.
- Show the modal popup with fields: Full Name, Work Email, Temporary Password, Role, 
  Department, and Designation.
- Click 'Cancel'.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Under 'Users & Staff', administrators provision team credentials, assign 
operational designations, and manage RBAC authorities with instant modal popups."

[ON SCREEN] In Sidebar, click 'Subscriptions'.
- On an active subscription, click the amber 'Pause' button.
- Show the confirmation dialog explaining that recurring billing will be halted.
- Click 'Cancel' or click 'Renew' to show the renewal cycle confirmation dialog.
[VOICEOVER]:
"In the Subscriptions Lifecycle engine, admins and staff can transition contracts 
through every state — Draft, Quotation, Confirmed, Active, Paused, or Closed — 
with dedicated audit notes and confirmation popups."


================================================================================
PART 2: STAFF OPERATIONAL DESK (~60 seconds)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN] Click the user avatar in the top right header, click 'Sign Out'.
[ON SCREEN] Click the 'Staff' one-click demo button, then click 'Sign in'.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Now let's switch to the Staff role to see operational desk governance."

[ON SCREEN] Land on Staff Operational Desk.
- Notice the top header badge reads 'STAFF OPERATIONAL DESK'.
- Highlight that sensitive financial reports and admin-only settings are hidden.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Staff members are greeted with an Operational Desk focused on active accounts, 
contract renewals, and overdue payment chase queues. Notice that high-level 
profit margins, MRR, and admin governance tabs are hidden for security."

[ON SCREEN] In Sidebar, click 'Invoices'.
- Click 'Record Payment' on an unpaid invoice.
- Show the payment method options (Bank Transfer, UPI, Cheque, Cash) and amount.
- Click 'Record Offline Payment'.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Staff can record manual offline payments — such as NEFT bank transfers or UPI — 
instantly reducing the customer's open balance, adjusting tax ledgers, and 
updating audit logs."


================================================================================
PART 3: CUSTOMER SELF-SERVICE PORTAL (~45 seconds)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN] Sign out from Staff and click the 'Customer' one-click demo button.
[ON SCREEN] Click 'Sign in' and land on /portal.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Finally, let's explore the Customer Self-Service Portal as John Doe from Acme Innovations."

[ON SCREEN] Customer Dashboard.
- Show the dark welcome banner: 'Welcome back, John Doe!' with company name.
- Point to 'Total Outstanding Balance' alert card and active subscriptions card.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Customers enjoy a branded self-service workspace. They can instantly check 
their current outstanding dues and active managed subscriptions."

[ON SCREEN] In navigation, click 'My Subscriptions'.
- Show the active Cloud ERP plan, monthly billing cadence, and next renewal date.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Under 'My Subscriptions', customers view their enrolled service plans, contracted 
quantities, and upcoming cycle renewal dates."

[ON SCREEN] In navigation, click 'Invoices & Payments'.
- Click 'Download PDF' icon on any invoice to show the downloaded PDF statement.
- Point to the 'Pay Now' button with the Razorpay integration.
[VOICEOVER]:
"Under 'Invoices & Payments', customers can view their complete statement history, 
download official GST-compliant PDF invoices, and settle balances online via 
our integrated Razorpay payment gateway."


================================================================================
PART 4: OUTRO (~15 seconds)
================================================================================

[ON SCREEN] Return to the landing page or portal dashboard.
[VOICEOVER]:
"From automated subscription lifecycles to multi-tier role-based access control 
and customer self-service payments, Brightloop provides an all-in-one recurring 
billing engine. Thank you for watching!"
